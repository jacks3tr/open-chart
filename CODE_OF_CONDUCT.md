# Code of Conduct

OpenChart welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Be respectful, constructive, and patient in issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces.

- Discuss the work and its evidence; do not attack people.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and unwanted sexual attention.
- Accept feedback in good faith and make room for other perspectives.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect contributors. Enforcement should be
proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For misconduct, contact a maintainer privately when a contact channel is
available. Otherwise, request a private contact in an issue without identifying
affected people or publishing sensitive details. GitHub's abuse-reporting tools
are also available for conduct that violates its platform policies.
